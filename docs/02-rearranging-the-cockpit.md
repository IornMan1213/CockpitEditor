# 2. Rearranging the cockpit (Layout tab)

The Layout tab lists everything in the cockpit that can be moved, sorted into **Displays**, **Instruments**, **Levers and
knobs** and **Switch panels**. The MiG-29 has 35 (every round gauge, the ADI, HSI, SPO-15, caution panel, gear lights and
lever, radar screen, knobs, console panels); the MiG-29SMT has 21 (the three big displays, UFCP, standby display, gear lights
and lever, master caution, knobs, console panels).

Anything you change gets a **•** next to its name.

## Select something

- Click it in the list, **or**
- click it in the cockpit.

It gets a yellow outline and its settings appear on the right.

## Move it somewhere else

1. Select it.
2. Press **Move** (or the **M** key). A yellow banner says "Click in the cockpit to place it".
3. Move the mouse over the cockpit: a blue outline shows where it would go.
4. Click. It sits flat on whatever you clicked: the panel, a console top, the side wall, the floor.
5. **Esc** cancels.

Its needles, lights and screen go with it and keep working in the game.

## Fine adjustments

| To | Do |
|---|---|
| Nudge 1 mm | arrow keys, or the arrow buttons in the right panel |
| Nudge 1 cm | Shift + arrow keys |
| Turn 5° | **Q** / **E** (Shift: 1°), or type in **Rotation** |
| Resize | **+** / **-**, or the **Size** slider (25 %–250 %) |
| Stick it back onto the surface behind it (after nudging it off a curve) | the round button in the middle of the nudge arrows |
| Hide it | **H**, the eye button in the list, or tick **Hidden** |
| Put it back exactly as it was | **Reset** |

A hidden instrument disappears completely in the game; the panel behind it is plain. Untick Hidden (or press H) to bring it back.

## Display pages (MiG-29SMT)

Under the list, **Display pages** has a tick box per built-in screen page (flight display, RWR/engines, stores, standby,
UFCP window). Untick one to leave that built-in screen dark, for example after you put that page on a screen of your own
([Screens](04-screens.md)). The screen's frame stays; hide the display in the list if you want the whole thing gone.

## Good to know

- You cannot copy an instrument (the game drives each one); add a screen or decal instead.
- Instruments can overlap if you put them on top of each other: check from the **Pilot** view.
- A decal does not follow an instrument you move later: move the decal too.
